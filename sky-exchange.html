How does Sky's in-play bet slip work? +
The bet slip floats at the bottom of your screen without covering the live feed or score. Tap any odd to auto-add it, adjust stake with +/- buttons, confirm with one more tap. From tap-to-placed: under 1.5 seconds.
Is Sky suitable for beginners? +
Absolutely. The onboarding shows you how back/lay works with visual examples, and the UI hides advanced features until you're ready. New users say they understood exchange betting within 10 minutes of signup.
