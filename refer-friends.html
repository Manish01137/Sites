REFERRAL FAQ

Do I need to share my Cricket ID with my friend? +
No. Your referral link is independent of your ID — it only identifies you for tracking purposes. Your friend creates their own Cricket ID with their own credentials.
When do I get paid? +
The ₹400 credit hits your wallet within 10 minutes of your friend's first deposit clearing. If they use UPI, the entire referral → signup → deposit → reward chain can finish in under 5 minutes.
What if my friend doesn't deposit? +
No reward is issued until the friend deposits at least ₹500. This is to prevent fake signups. Once they deposit, the reward is irrevocable — even if they withdraw immediately.
Is there a cap on total earnings? +
No cap. Our top referrer last month earned ₹3.2 lakh by bringing 480 signups. We actually pay bonuses on top of the base ₹400 rate once you cross 10 friends — see the table above.
Can I withdraw the referral credit immediately? +
Yes. Referral earnings have zero rollover requirement. They're treated as regular cash. Withdraw anytime via UPI/PhonePe/GPay or use them to place bets — your choice.
